A few words of gratitude to the people who contributed to this journey.

01

Thank you

A year made easier by others

This first year of teacher education has brought new experiences, challenges and friendships. I wouldn't have made it through quite the same way without the people who supported me along the way.

First of all, I want to thank my mom and dad for always supporting me and helping me in every way they can. I also want to thank my sister, who, even from another country, always checks in on me and has helped me more times than I can count. Knowing I can always count on them has made this whole experience much easier.

I can't leave out Ceniza, my cat, who has kept me company through countless hours of homework and studying. She is always waiting to greet me enthusiastically whenever I get home, sleeps with me and spends most of her day by my side. Having her around makes even the longest study sessions a little less lonely.

I also want to thank Cristóbal and the other cats at CERP, who are always among the first to welcome us whenever we arrive in Colonia. They've made our visits feel a little more like home. Cristóbal has even joined some of our lessons, sitting with us as if he were just another student!

I'm also really thankful for my classmates at IFD and CERP, especially my fellow English students from Mercedes: Manuela, Ximena, Eugenia and Milagros. Over the course of this year, we've gone from being classmates to becoming a very close group of friends. They've been there for me both academically and personally, and I honestly don't think this year would have been the same without them. Getting to know them has definitely been one of the highlights of my year.

I also want to give a special mention to Noel. She's a second-year English student, but she's taking some first-year subjects at IFD, so we've had the chance to get to know each other even though we don't share classes at CERP. We've become friends, and she's helped me tremendously in many different ways. I also had the opportunity to interview her for this portfolio, which gave me the chance to learn more about her experiences as a student and future teacher.

Finally, I want to thank Karina, Milagros' mom, who kindly drove us to CERP in Colonia in her own car and patiently waited for us while we attended our study day. It was incredibly generous of her, and it made things much easier for all of us.

Looking back, I realise that this whole experience would have been much more difficult without all these people (and kitties!) by my side. I'm lucky to have them, and I'm genuinely grateful for everything they've done for me throughout this first year.

FL.

E. Federico López · Introducción al Campo Profesional · 2026
