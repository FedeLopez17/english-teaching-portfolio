01

On screen

Movies

FILM 01

Like Stars on Earth (2007)

Directed by Aamir Khan

We watched this film in Pedagogy class. It follows Ishaan, a boy whose difficulties at school are misunderstood by the adults around him. It reminded me that every student is different and that we can't expect everyone to learn in the same way. I'd like to be a teacher who takes the time to understand students, recognize their needs and look beyond their academic performance.

FILM 02

Dead Poets Society (1989)

Directed by Peter Weir

I watched this one on my own and really enjoyed it. I like John Keating's approach to teaching and the relationship he builds with his students. He encourages them to think for themselves and see life differently. That's the kind of influence I'd like to have: not just teaching a subject, but helping students discover their own voices and perspectives.

02

Captured moments

Photographs

I also wanted to include a couple of photographs that mean something to me. They're not particularly remarkable pictures, but they remind me of little moments I wouldn't want to forget.

PHOTO 01

Buen Viaje

I've driven past this painted house countless times on my way to Colonia and back to Mercedes. It simply says “Buen Viaje,” but it's one of those little details that somehow stays with you. I like that it has become part of my routine and memories without me even noticing.

PHOTO 02

Rainbow

I took this picture one day while driving back to Mercedes after class. We saw a rainbow, stopped on the side of the road and took a few pictures. It was an unexpected moment that made the trip memorable. I think it's important to appreciate the little things that make an ordinary day special.

03

Listening

Music

Music is a big part of my life. I've been playing guitar for a few years now and I'm really passionate about it. I've included songs whose lyrics I relate to, as well as instrumental pieces I enjoy listening to and playing. Some bring back memories, others help me make sense of how I feel, and some are simply beautiful to me.

Songs

Vienna

Billy Joel

Forever Young

Alphaville

Time in a Bottle

Jim Croce

These songs make me think about time, growing up and not rushing through life. I relate to their nostalgic but hopeful undertone. I'm 25, which makes me a few years older than most of my closest friends in class. Sometimes that difference makes me feel a little out of place, and these songs help me put things into perspective. I also tend to worry about the future and whether I'm making the right decisions. They remind me that there's no need to have everything figured out and that everyone has their own path.

We're Going to Be Friends

The White Stripes

Strawberry Fields Forever

The Beatles

With a Little Help from My Friends

The Beatles

There's something almost magical about these songs. They bring back a sense of childhood, imagination and friendship, and make me feel hopeful about the future. They also remind me of how important it is to have people around who support you. I'd love to create a classroom where students can build that kind of connection with one another.

Mapa Tesoro

Nicolás Ibarburu

I really like its lyrics about listening, getting through difficult times, forgiving and learning to let go. It makes me reflect on personal growth and finding my own way, without holding on to things that no longer help me move forward.

Construyendo Recuerdos

Trío Ventana

I love its appreciation for simple things, nature and the experiences that shape us. It reminds me of what I mentioned when talking about the rainbow: how meaningful the simple, unexpected moments in life can be.

Permiso y Prometo

Las Pastillas del Abuelo

This song makes me think about the relationship between a teacher and a student: offering guidance and support while also allowing someone the freedom to grow, make mistakes and find their own way.

El Aprendiz

Cuarteto de Nos

This song was particularly important to me during a certain period of my life. It brings back memories of who I was then and reminds me how much I've changed since. I still have a lot to learn, and I hope I never lose that willingness to keep growing.

Promise

Slash feat. Chris Cornell

I relate to its lyrics about identity and belonging. It also gives me a feeling of strength and makes me think about overcoming challenges without losing who I am.

Instrumental Music

Instrumental music is just as important to me. I enjoy pieces that are nostalgic, melancholic, peaceful or emotionally intense. Some are particularly meaningful because I play them myself. The classical guitar pieces, especially, help me unwind and manage my feelings. Listening to music can also completely change my mood, give me energy or motivate me when I need it.

Playing music has taught me patience, discipline and how to express myself. I'd love to bring some of that into my teaching, perhaps through songs, guitar or other creative activities. Music has been such an important part of my own life that I'd like to share some of that experience with my students.

Just because

A Little Something Different

VIDEO GAME SOUNDTRACK

Cara Mia Addio! — Portal 2

This piece plays during the ending of Portal 2, one of my favourite video games. It's a somewhat challenging puzzle game, so reaching the ending feels incredibly rewarding. The first time I experienced it, I was genuinely moved. It felt like a well-earned reward after the whole journey.

I think video games can offer much more than entertainment. They can challenge us, tell meaningful stories and create experiences we remember for years. I'd like to stay open to the things my students enjoy and not underestimate the potential they have as sources of inspiration, creativity and learning.
