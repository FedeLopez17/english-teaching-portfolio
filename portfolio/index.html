INTRODUCCIÓN AL CAMPO PROFESIONAL · 2026

Becoming
a teacher.

A portfolio of research, reflection and first steps into the professional field of education.

This portfolio brings together a research and introspection task developed throughout the course. It explores the educational system, the local educational community, the work of English teachers and my own ideas about teaching. It is also an opportunity to reflect on what I have learned so far and the kind of teacher I would like to become.
